Who We Are

Manassas Energy Labs is an independent research initiative focused on frontier questions in the energy sector. Our team combines technical training, data analysis, and industry insight to examine how new technologies, economic forces, and infrastructure constraints are transforming energy systems. We publish research designed to help decision-makers understand not only what is happening in the energy sector today, but what structural changes may shape the next decade.
